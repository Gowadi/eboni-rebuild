The Story

Following severe storm activity, Eboni experienced significant impact to her home and living situation.

In times of sudden crisis, displacement creates immediate financial strain—from temporary housing and pet care for Boone to replacing damaged essentials and securing physical repairs.

Eboni has always been a pillar of care and creative energy in our community. This hub serves as a central place to share progress, offer aid, and help get Eboni back on solid ground.
